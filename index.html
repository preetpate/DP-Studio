FAQ

Common questions.

What is included in the ₹3,000 Starter package? +

The Starter package includes up to 3 pages, responsive design, modern UI, contact section, basic animations, 1 revision round and project documentation. Additional pages or features can affect the final price — this is always discussed before we start.

How much does a website cost? +

Projects start from ₹3,000+ depending on pages, design complexity and features required. Final pricing is discussed and agreed before any work begins.

How long does it take? +

It depends on the project size and features. We provide an estimated timeline before development starts so you always know what to expect.

Is hosting included? +

Deployment assistance is included for eligible projects (₹10,000+). Domain and hosting charges are separate and depend on your setup.

Can I request changes after the website is built? +

Yes. Revision rounds are included in every package (Starter: 1 round, Business: 2 rounds). The scope will be discussed before development begins.

Will I get the source code? +

Yes. Source code is included in the final project delivery for all packages unless otherwise agreed for a specific custom project.

Do you provide documentation? +

Yes. A project report covering overview, features, technologies, setup, screenshots and future improvements can be provided with your project.

How do I get started? +

Click "Start a Project" anywhere on this page, fill in a short form and we'll get back to you with the next steps.

Don't know what website you need?

That's okay. Tell us about your business and we'll help you figure out what kind of website makes sense.
