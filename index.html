FAQ

Common questions.

What is included in the ₹3,000 Starter package?+

Up to 3 pages, responsive design, modern UI, contact section, basic animations, 1 revision round and project documentation. Additional pages or features are quoted separately — always discussed before we start.

How much does a website cost?+

Projects start from ₹3,000+ depending on pages, complexity and features. Final pricing is agreed before any work begins.

How long does it take?+

Depends on the project size. We provide an estimated timeline before development starts so you always know what to expect.

Is hosting included?+

Deployment assistance is included for projects ₹10,000+. Domain and hosting charges are separate.

Can I request changes after the website is built?+

Yes. Revision rounds are included — Starter: 1 round, Business: 2 rounds. Scope is agreed before development starts.

Will I get the source code?+

Yes. Source code is included in the final delivery for all packages unless otherwise agreed for a specific custom project.

Do you provide documentation?+

Yes. A project report covering overview, features, technologies, setup, screenshots and future improvements can be provided.

How do I get started?+

Click "Start a Project" anywhere on this page, fill in a short form and we'll get back to you with the next steps.

Don't know what website you need?

That's okay. Tell us about your business and we'll help you figure out what kind of website makes sense.
