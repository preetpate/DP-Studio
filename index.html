FAQ

Common questions.

How much does a website cost? +

Projects start from ₹3,000+ depending on pages, design complexity and features required. Final pricing is discussed before any work begins.

How long does it take? +

It depends on the project size and features. We provide an estimated timeline before development starts so you always know what to expect.

Is hosting included? +

Deployment assistance is included for eligible projects (₹10,000+). Domain and hosting charges are separate and depend on your setup.

Can I request changes after the website is built? +

Yes. The revision process and scope will be discussed and agreed upon before development begins.

Will I get the source code? +

Yes. Source code is handed over as part of the final delivery based on the selected package.

Do you provide documentation? +

Yes. A project report/documentation covering overview, features, technologies, setup, screenshots and future improvements can be provided with your project.

How do I get started? +

Click "Start a Project" anywhere on this page. Fill in a short form telling us about your project and we'll get back to you with the next steps.

Don't know what website you need?

That's okay. Tell us about your business and we'll help you figure out what kind of website makes sense.
